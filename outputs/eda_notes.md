**1. Is spend symmetric?** No. Skewness is 4.61, mean INR 270 against median INR 135: a few expensive electronics orders pull the mean up, so the median describes a typical order better.

**2. Are there outliers?** Yes: 897 orders exceed the IQR upper fence (INR 645). They are genuine high-value purchases, not errors, so they are kept.

**3. Where does revenue come from?** Hyderabad earns the most overall (INR 528,590); Electronics contributes 41% of revenue.

**4. What drives order value?** Linear relationships are weak: quantity (r=0.09), discount (r=-0.10) and delivery time (r=-0.00) barely track amount. Product price, not captured here, likely dominates.

Correlation does not establish causation.
